## Failed / surprises

- Most wattmeter-capable, multi-NIC Grid'5000 clusters expose exactly two
  Ethernet interfaces, not three. A two-interface fallback was first drafted
  into ADR-11, then rejected by the project owner the same day (see
  Decisions).
- Verified on *Monitoring Using Kwollect* (metric availability table) and
  the Grenoble/Luxembourg hardware pages: the only three-interface clusters
  are `servan` (Grenoble, exotic, 2 nodes), `kinovis` (Grenoble, `abaca`
  production queue, 6 nodes, 2 × L40S) and `vianden` (Luxembourg, exotic,
  1 node, 8 × MI300X). **None has a dedicated wattmeter**; all expose
  `bmc_node_power_watt` (5 s). Keeping three NICs would have made the BMC
  the external reference on Grid'5000, which led to the second revision
  (see Decisions).

## Decisions

- ADR-11: Grid'5000 is the primary measurement platform; the home mini-PC
  bench is secondary (development, calibration, BIOS-level mitigations,
  consumer-hardware cross-check). ADR-02 and ADR-04 amended.
- Node selection, first revision: restricted to three-interface clusters
  (user decision), with `servan` as working candidate and BMC power as the
  external reference.
- Node selection, second revision (user decision, same day): **energy
  precision takes precedence over one NIC per network.** Node selection
  targets wattmeter clusters with two usable interfaces (`gros-[41-76]`
  Nancy, `chirop` Lille; `neowise` last resort); management on the first
  NIC, N3 and N6 sharing the second as VLANs/subnets (declared deviation
  from ADR-04). External reference is `wattmetre_power_watt` (1 s), with
  `bmc_node_power_watt` as cross-check. The three-interface/BMC option
  (`servan`, `kinovis`, `vianden`) is recorded as a rejected alternative in
  ADR-11. ADR-04, ADR-07, RM-02, architecture, methodology, test plan, data
  dictionary, figures and the Grid'5000 example inventory updated.
- Affiliation stays Télécom Paris.
- Paper track: no external deadline; 5–7 months from Nov 2026, venue to be
  chosen; KubeCon EU 2027 remains a dissemination milestone only.
- A short Grid'5000 acceptance reservation (E1.7) is a prerequisite of G1.
- Everything in English; repository is public; real inventories and
  credentials are git-ignored.
